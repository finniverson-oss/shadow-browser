# Landing page assets

The landing design, base stylesheet, and supplied logo are adapted from the
Magma page referenced in the user's recording:
https://magma2-mantle.laneesports.com/

The page uses a local copy of particles.js 2.0.0 by Vincent Garreau (MIT):
https://github.com/VincentGarreau/particles.js
The original library's notices are preserved in particles.min.js.

DM Sans is distributed under the SIL Open Font License:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans
Material Symbols is distributed under the Apache License 2.0:
https://github.com/google/material-design-icons

Fonts, icons, animation code, and the logo are embedded in the launcher, so
the landing page does not rely on the reference website being reachable.
The landing controls use ShadowBrowser's viewer and settings.
